> My research has three strands. **Reality and its distortions**: visual illusions, deception and lie detection, misinformation and fake news, beliefs about AI-generated content, fiction, and altered states of consciousness.  **The body in cognition**: how interoception, physiological states and emotion regulation shape what we take to be real. **Methods:** Bayesian statistics, computational models and psychophysiology, as well as the development of new software, measures and paradigms, released as open source (easystats, NeuroKit, cogmod) to support open and rigorous science.

- **psycho**
  - **Description:** R package for psychologists, neuropsychologists and
    neuroscientists, built around publication-oriented reporting. Its central
    function, analyze(), converted the output of common statistical procedures
    into formatted, APA-style text ready to paste into a manuscript, with the aim
    of standardizing statistical reporting and removing a common source of
    transcription errors. Also provided data standardization, signal detection
    theory indices, and neuropsychological single-case tests against a control
    group. The first attempt at the automated-reporting idea that later became the
    report package and, more broadly, the easystats project.
  - **Language:** R
  - **Dates:** 2017-2019
  - **Role:** Sole author and maintainer.
  - **Repository:** https://github.com/neuropsychology/psycho.R

  - **Status:** Deprecated in favour of report, part of easystats; most of the

## Side Projects

### NeuropsyXart

- **Type:** Art project
- **Link:** https://dominiquemakowski.github.io/NeuropsyXart/
- **Description:**
  - A project bridging my interests in art, attractors (complexity and non-linear dynamics), and physiology. 
  - A form of art involving the subject's own physiological activity. The subject undergoes a mental experience while their heart or brain activity is recorded (electrocardiography, ECG, or electroencephalography, EEG); the signal is then embedded as a trajectory in multidimensional state space and its attractor is plotted as the artwork. The image is therefore a property of that person's physiological state rather than of the artist, who "is merely but a facilitator, a tool".
- **Exhibitions:**
  - Brain Art Exhibition, "The Multifaceted Brain: Adaptation and Diversity", 29th Annual Meeting of the Organization for Human Brain Mapping (OHBM), 22-26 July 2023, Montreal, Canada. Selected for exhibition in the "written text or still image" category. See https://ohbm-brainart.github.io/exhibit/exhibit2023.html and https://sites.google.com/view/brainart-exhibit-2023
