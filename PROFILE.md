# Dominique Makowski — Profile (Ground Truth)

Exhaustive, plain-text record of everything about Dominique Makowski. This file is
the **source of truth**: nothing is ever shortened, curated or deleted here.
Rendered outputs (`DominiqueMakowski_CV.Rmd`, lab profiles, biosketches, funder
portals, LinkedIn) are *views* on this file and may select, compress or reorder —
but they must not be the only place a fact lives.

**Conventions**

- `##` = category. `###` = one entry. Entries are ordered newest first.
- Every entry is a flat list of `- **Field:** value` lines. Field names are reused
  across entries so they can be pattern-matched; fields that do not apply are
  omitted rather than left empty.
- Dates: `YYYY` for a point in time, `YYYY-YYYY` for a span, `YYYY-present` if
  ongoing. Always full four-digit years, never `2014 - 18`.
- Sub-grouping within a category is expressed as a `**Domain:**` or `**Type:**`
  field, not as nested headings, so filtering stays mechanical.
- Names of people keep their title and a link where one is known.
- Plain text and plain ASCII punctuation. No LaTeX, no R, no escaping. Accented
  characters in proper nouns are fine.
- Write things in full: an acronym always appears next to its expansion.
