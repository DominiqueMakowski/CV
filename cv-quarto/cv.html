Trained as a clinical neuropsychologist and CBT psychotherapist, I am an assistant professor at the University of Sussex, where I head the Reality Bending Lab. My research has three strands. Reality and its distortions: visual illusions, deception and lie detection, misinformation and fake news, beliefs about AI-generated content, fiction, and altered states of consciousness. The body in cognition: how interoception, physiological states and emotion regulation shape what we take to be real. Methods: Bayesian statistics, computational models and psychophysiology, as well as the development of new software, measures and paradigms, released as open source (easystats, NeuroKit, cogmod) to support open and rigorous science.

NeuroKit130k downloads/month

Signal processing for ECG, PPG, EDA, EMG, EOG and EEG, from peak detection to heart rate variability and complexity indices. 2,355 stars and dozens of contributors. SIPS Commendation (2024).

Python · Creator and maintainer · 2020 - Present

Generates visual illusions parametrically, separating illusion strength from the objective difference between the targets, so that sensitivity can be measured continuously.

cogmod

R · Sole author · 2024 - Present

Bayesian models of reaction times, choices and ratings - drift diffusion, linear ballistic accumulator, racing diffusion - as custom brms families.
