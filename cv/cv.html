Trained as a clinical neuropsychologist and CBT psychotherapist, I am an assistant professor at the University of Sussex, where I head the Reality Bending Lab. My research has three strands. Reality and its distortions: beliefs about AI-generated content, visual illusions, deception, misinformation and fake news, fiction, and altered states of consciousness. The body in cognition: how interoception, physiological states and emotion regulation shape what we take to be real. Methods: Bayesian statistics, computational models and psychophysiology, as well as the development of new software, measures and paradigms, released as open source (easystats, NeuroKit, cogmod) to support open and rigorous science.

Ten R packages for the post-processing of statistical models: parameters, effect sizes, diagnostics, Bayesian indices, marginal effects, reporting. Used in over 10,000 publications. SIPS Mission Award (2023).

NeuroKit130k downloads/month

Python · Creator · 2017 - Present

Most popular Python package for signal processing (ECG, PPG, EDA, ...), from peak detection to heart rate variability and complexity indices. 2,355 stars and dozens of contributors. SIPS Commendation (2024).

Python · Creator · 2020 - Present

Generates visual illusions parametrically, separating illusion strength from the objective difference between the targets, so that sensitivity can be measured continuously.
